## 3. Cybersecurity News: ShinyHunters Attack on the FBI (September 2026)

In September 2026, the cybercriminal group ShinyHunters claimed responsibility for an attack on the FBI's jobs website, FBIJobs.gov. The group claimed it had accessed sensitive information belonging to FBI employees and job applicants. The FBI stated that it was investigating the claims of unauthorised activity and possible exposure of personal information.

This incident shows that even important government organisations can face cybersecurity threats. It highlights the importance of protecting sensitive data, securing third-party systems, and investigating security incidents quickly.
